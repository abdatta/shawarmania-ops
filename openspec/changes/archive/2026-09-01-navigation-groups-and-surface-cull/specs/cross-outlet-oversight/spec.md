## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Two outlets are compared over a chosen period

**Reason**: The comparison surface never ran on live data, and `#13
owner-console-live` — which would have made it real — is withdrawn (owner
decision, 2026-08-31).

**The rest of this capability stays and is used daily.** The owner console
showing every outlet side by side, figures derived from recorded rows, the outlet
switcher that can never widen past what the caller may read, and the read-only
day view an outlet opens to are all untouched. What goes is the
period-over-period comparison of two outlets against each other, and nothing
else.

### Requirement: Reports summarise a period on screen and produce no file

**Reason**: Withdrawn with the comparison above. The no-file property it carried
is not weakened by its removal: there is now no export path at all, which is
strictly stronger than a surface that promised not to build one.
