# Proposal: Preparing Order Pipeline

> **Model**: Opus · **Wave**: D · **Depends on**: #10 · **Gate**: an order taken at the counter lands in Preparing, reaches Unpaid Prepared Orders by Mark prepared, returns by Reprepare while unpaid, and reaches Bills only once prepared and paid — every transition landing in the correct section on the tablet, offline included, with a paid-but-not-yet-prepared order staying in Preparing wearing its Paid marker; within five minutes of payment the originating tablet can take the payment back (bill voided, order reopened) or cancel after payment (warned first; bill voided with reason, order cancelled), both refused by the database outside the window and both impossible as direct table writes, proved by hand-crafted requests; every post-settlement void stamps a structured kind with actor and reason, and manager billing history marks Cancelled after paid without counting it in any total; line editing is refused on a paid order by the database and the interface alike; the workspace reads Menu | Bills this shift (composer overlaying it) | Preparing over Unpaid Prepared Orders, with the rename carried everywhere user-visible and both columns' shimmers reshaped to match; a one-item pipeline card stands about 100px tall so at least six fit the rail at landscape-tablet height without scrolling, its uncommon actions behind a kebab whose menu rows stay touch-safe; stage changes animate as a flight between sections with shimmer placeholders opening space at both ends, suppressed under reduced-motion; the demo biller sees local references until simulated delivery, an outlet-wide pipeline that includes another tablet's order, and a queue that drains without waiting for a network event; offline order → prepare → pay replays exactly once online; and the four-role demo walkthrough still walks.

## Why

The billers asked for a different flow, and their ask exposes a real gap in today's counter: **"Order" and "preparing" are currently the same moment.** A saved order sits in Open orders among everything else, so nobody can see at a glance what the kitchen is still making versus what is waiting only for money. The outlet wants a Preparing list — important enough that mirroring it on a kitchen screen later is already anticipated — and wants the money step to be explicit and reversible in the seconds after it happens.

Three further frictions compound it. The Open orders cards are shaped like receipts (per-line prices, padded quantity badges, a three-button row per card) and cost 160px for a one-item order, so barely four fit the rail. "Open orders" has quietly become the wrong name for a section that will now exclude preparing work. And the demo biller sees a counter a real tablet does not: instant order numbers instead of local references, a device-scoped list instead of the outlet-wide one live adapters serve, and a seeded pending bill that never drains.

## What Changes

- **An order gains a preparation axis independent of payment.** `orders.prepared_at` (nullable timestamptz): null means still preparing. The status enum is untouched — `status` remains the money lifecycle, because *paid but still cooking* must be representable.
- **Two new typed commands** ride the existing outbox: `set_order_preparation` (mark prepared / reprepare, carrying an explicit flag and timestamp) and the payment unwinds `void_order_payment` (bill voided, order reopened) and `cancel_paid_order` (bill voided, order cancelled). Both unwinds are atomic compounds guarded by the same five-minute clock that already gates tender corrections, chained behind the payment they reverse.
- **Append-only grows one deliberate grace clause**: the originating tablet may void its own bill through these commands inside the window. Direct table writes stay impossible for every role; outside the window, unwinding stays manager-only exactly as today.
- **Every post-settlement void stamps a structured kind** (`manager_void`, `counter_unpay`, `cancelled_after_paid`) with actor and reason. Manager billing history shows Cancelled-after-paid as a marker; totals keep excluding voided bills automatically.
- **The workspace rearranges**: Bills this shift becomes the middle column's default content — shift totals on top, collapsed bills with their countdown chip beneath — with the composer overlaying it whenever an item is tapped or an order is opened for edit. The right rail becomes the pure pipeline: Preparing over a labelled divider over **Unpaid Prepared Orders** (the rename, applied everywhere user-visible). The rail stops mixing money history into work-in-flight, which is also precisely the slice a future kitchen screen mirrors.
- **Pipeline cards become compact tickets**: one meta line, plain quantity prefixes without per-line prices (the total stays prominent; line amounts remain on the bill and in the composer), one primary action that names the section's next step — Mark prepared or Mark paid — and a kebab holding Edit, Reprepare, Cancel, Un-pay and Cancel after paid as each state allows. A PAID chip marks paid-but-unprepared cards. Line editing locks once an order is paid.
- **Stage changes animate.** Cards fly between sections and columns on a FLIP layer with shimmer placeholders opening space at both ends, within a ~300ms budget, suppressed under reduced motion — generalising the dock animation the edit flow already has.
- **Demo parity lands with the change**: mock commands queue uniformly (local reference until simulated delivery), open lists go outlet-wide with a second fabricated tablet's order in the seeds, and the seeded pending bill drains on subscribe instead of waiting forever.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `counter-billing`: the workspace's three columns hold menu / bills-and-composer / preparing-and-unpaid-prepared; pipeline cards compact to one primary action plus kebab; the counter can un-pay or cancel-after-paid within the grace window; post-settlement voids carry structured kinds surfaced in manager history; the section renames to Unpaid Prepared Orders.
- `order-lifecycle`: an order carries preparation state independent of payment; preparation commands obey the terminal-state lock; reprepare dies at the bills border.
- `billing-command-contract`: `set_order_preparation`, `void_order_payment` and `cancel_paid_order` join the versioned command vocabulary with replay, guard and receipt semantics identical to their siblings.
- `billing-delivery`: unwinds chain behind the payment they reverse and project onto local reads before delivery, like every other command.

One capability the work touches carries no requirement change: `cross-outlet-oversight` reads manager billing history unchanged, since the marker renders inside the existing history surface rather than adding a console requirement.
