# Customer Analytics

**Type**: Feature · **Status**: Owner asked to record, 9 October 2026 · **Area**: Analytics / Customers

Customers moved under Analytics on 9 October 2026 because it already answers who the regulars are, how often they come and what they spend. The owner asked for its analytics to grow later, and for these ideas to be kept rather than built yet. Each is one outlet at a time, like the rest of Analytics, and reads the same settled bills, so none of them needs a new record kept at the counter.

- **Regulars going quiet.** A customer who usually comes every week or so and has not been seen for three of their usual gaps — a win-back list, with how long since their last visit and what they usually spend. "Usually" is the customer's own median gap, not a fixed number of days.
- **New and returning.** For a period, how much revenue and how many bills came from first-time customers against returning ones, compared with earlier equal periods like Sales.
- **Do they come back.** Of the customers first seen in a week or a month, what share came back within 7, 14 and 30 days — the plainest measure of whether the food, or a promotion, is keeping people.
- **Gold against everyone else.** Average bill, visits a month and spend of gold members against other named customers, and how many earned points are actually spent. This is what tells the owner whether gold and points are worth what they cost.
- **Top customers for a period**, by spend and by visits, with each one's favourite dish.
- **When regulars come.** The weekday and hour pattern of returning customers against first-timers.

Constraints to settle before promoting:

- Only bills that carry a customer's number count, so every figure is about **named** customers, a share of all sales that has to be shown beside it or the figures mislead.
- A mistyped or reassigned number splits or merges a person; until merge and split exist (see [Customer Loyalty And Cross-Outlet Insights](./customer-loyalty-and-cross-outlet-insights.md)), a figure per customer is only as good as the numbers typed.
- The same egress rule as Items and Sales: aggregates per view, never a list of every customer's bills to the phone.
- Business-wide versions, across outlets, belong to the cross-outlet note above rather than here.

**Trigger to promote:** the owner asks for any one of these on the Customers page, or Kalyani Cafe has three months of named-customer bills to make the return figures mean something.
