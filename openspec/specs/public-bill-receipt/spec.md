# Public Bill Receipt

## Purpose

Public receipt links expose exactly one current bill, with how it was served
and the points it earned, used and left. The receipt names no customer: a bill
with a customer attached shows only the last four digits of its recorded number
and whether its customer was gold at that outlet. No link weakens outlet
tenancy, alters billing history, or stores a rendered document.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Every bill is linkable, automatically, from the moment the server has it

Every bill SHALL carry exactly one public link record, created by the database at
the moment the bill row is inserted, without any application code, share step or
counter action.

The link's token SHALL be generated by the database from a cryptographically
secure source, SHALL be URL-safe, and SHALL be unique across all bills. Its length
SHALL NOT be fixed by a constraint, so that a longer token may be minted later
without invalidating any link already issued.

The token SHALL NOT be derived from, and SHALL NOT be, the bill's identifier, its
bill number, or any function of the bill's contents.

Every bill that exists when this capability lands SHALL receive a link in the same
migration, so that no bill is unshareable for having been rung too early.

#### Scenario: A bill is rung

- **WHEN** a bill row is inserted by any path
- **THEN** a public link record exists for it with a unique token, and nothing on
  the counter waited for it

#### Scenario: A bill settled before this capability existed

- **WHEN** a bill rung before this change is opened
- **THEN** it has a link, indistinguishable from one rung afterwards

#### Scenario: A bill still in the outbox

- **WHEN** a bill has been accepted locally but has not yet reached the server
- **THEN** it has no link, and no surface offers one for it

### Requirement: A link is revocable, one bill at a time, and never expires

A link SHALL be revocable. Revoking one SHALL take effect immediately, SHALL be
permanent for that token, and SHALL affect no other bill's link.

A revoked bill SHALL be able to receive a fresh link, so that a customer who still
needs a receipt is not permanently refused one.

A link SHALL NOT expire on the passage of time.

Revoking a link SHALL NOT modify the bill, and no operation on a link SHALL be
capable of modifying a bill.

#### Scenario: A link is revoked

- **WHEN** a bill's link is revoked and the old URL is opened
- **THEN** it is refused, and every other bill's link continues to work

#### Scenario: A receipt kept for a year

- **WHEN** a customer opens a link a year after the bill was rung, and it was never
  revoked
- **THEN** the receipt is served

### Requirement: The public receipt names no customer

The receipt SHALL NOT show the customer's name, in whole or in part, in any form.

The receipt SHALL NOT show the customer's phone number, or any part of it, except
the last four digits of the number the bill recorded.

The receipt SHALL show the last four digits of the recorded number, and SHALL show
that the customer was a gold member at the bill's outlet, **only for a bill that
has a customer attached**. A bill with no customer attached, including every bill
rung before customers were identified by their number, SHALL show neither, whatever
text the bill carries in its name and phone columns.

Both facts SHALL be read from the bill's own stored values, never from the customer
directory or from current membership, so that a later change to the directory or a
revoked membership does not change a receipt already issued.

The full phone number SHALL NOT leave the database in the receipt's payload. The
omission SHALL be the reader function's own projection, not a renderer choosing
what to show.

The receipt SHALL NOT show the reason a bill was cancelled, and the reader SHALL
NOT return it [owner, 2026-09-30]: it is the outlet's own note. A cancelled bill
SHALL still read as cancelled, unmistakably.

The receipt SHALL NOT show any other person's identity: not the biller, not the
approving manager, not the till.

*Why this reads as it does.* A receipt link is a bearer token in a URL: whoever
holds it, forwarded or misdelivered, sees the bill. The rule since #54 has been
that a leaked link costs the customer one order and never a person, and that still
holds, because the name is what identifies a person and the name is never shown.
Four digits of a number, and that its owner is gold at one outlet, let the
customer confirm the receipt is theirs; they do not tell a stranger who that is.

#54 also declined the four digits so that they could one day serve as a second
factor. That option is spent: no check SHALL be built on the last four digits,
because every receipt prints them. The name was proposed for the receipt a second
time, and refused, because receipts are now sent to numbers keyed at a counter,
and a name would turn every wrong digit into the disclosure of a person.

#### Scenario: A bill carrying a name and a phone, with a customer attached

- **WHEN** a bill whose customer gave the number ending 0042 is served as a receipt
- **THEN** the page, its counter view and the PDF show the last four digits, 0042
- **AND** neither the name nor any other digit of the number appears anywhere in
  the payload, the page or the PDF

#### Scenario: A gold member's bill

- **WHEN** a bill rung while its customer was gold at the bill's outlet is served
- **THEN** the receipt says the customer was gold at that outlet

#### Scenario: Gold ended after the sale

- **WHEN** that customer's gold is revoked, or expires, and the receipt is opened
  again
- **THEN** it still says the customer was gold at that outlet

#### Scenario: A bill with no customer attached

- **WHEN** a bill rung without a number, or a bill rung before customers were
  identified that carries a typed name and phone but no customer, is served
- **THEN** the receipt shows no digits and no gold mark

#### Scenario: A link reaches the wrong person

- **WHEN** a link is opened by somebody other than the customer it was meant for
- **THEN** what they can learn is one order, the last four digits of a number and
  whether its owner was gold at that outlet, and nothing that names any person

### Requirement: The receipt states what was charged, exactly as it was stored

The receipt SHALL show the outlet, the bill number, the business date and the time
of sale, every line with its quantity and the unit price that line snapshotted,
every discount recorded on the bill as its own line naming its basis, the rounding
line, the total, and the payment allocation across every method used.

The receipt SHALL show the list price a line snapshotted, and SHALL NOT show a
reduced unit price.

Every monetary figure the receipt shows SHALL be a stored value formatted for
display. The receipt SHALL NOT recompute a subtotal, a discount, a rounding
amount, or a total.

The receipt SHALL be a receipt and SHALL NOT resemble a tax invoice: no GSTIN, no
tax breakup, and no tax line while bills are recorded as carrying no tax.

#### Scenario: A discounted bill

- **WHEN** a bill carrying a menu discount and a bill discount is served
- **THEN** each discount reads as its own line naming what it was, the lines show
  their list prices, and the rounding line and total are those the bill stored

#### Scenario: A bill discounted in full

- **WHEN** a bill whose discount reached its whole subtotal is served
- **THEN** the receipt shows the giveaway, the rounding that carried it to one
  rupee, and a one rupee total, rendered as the honest figures they are

#### Scenario: A split payment

- **WHEN** a bill settled across two tender methods is served
- **THEN** both allocations are shown with their amounts

### Requirement: The receipt is built when it is asked for, and is never stored

The receipt SHALL be generated from the bill's current stored state at the moment
of the request. No rendered receipt, in any format, SHALL be persisted.

A bill voided after its link was issued SHALL be served as cancelled, stated
unmistakably, and SHALL NOT be served as a valid-looking receipt.

A bill whose tender allocation was corrected SHALL be served with the corrected
allocation.

#### Scenario: A bill is voided after the link was sent

- **WHEN** the customer opens a link to a bill that has since been voided
- **THEN** the receipt states that the bill was cancelled

#### Scenario: Tender is corrected after the link was sent

- **WHEN** the customer reopens a link to a bill whose payment split was corrected
- **THEN** the corrected split is shown

### Requirement: A downloadable PDF is served from its own address

The receipt SHALL offer a download of the same receipt as a PDF, and SHALL NOT
begin that download without the reader asking for it.

The PDF SHALL be served from its own URL as an ordinary navigation, with a PDF
content type and a recognisable filename. It SHALL NOT be assembled in the reader's
browser or delivered through a script-generated object URL.

The PDF SHALL carry the same figures as the page, and SHALL be generated on demand
and never stored.

#### Scenario: A customer downloads their receipt

- **WHEN** the reader asks for the download
- **THEN** a PDF is served from its own address with a recognisable filename

#### Scenario: The page is opened

- **WHEN** a receipt link is opened
- **THEN** the receipt is displayed and no download begins on its own

### Requirement: Every refusal is identical

A request carrying a token that is unknown, malformed, revoked, or belongs to no
bill SHALL be answered with one and the same refusal, in the same words and with
the same status, disclosing nothing about which case occurred and nothing about
whether any bill exists.

The public endpoint SHALL be disableable in its entirety without a deployment, and
while disabled SHALL answer every request with that same refusal.

#### Scenario: An invented token

- **WHEN** a request carries a token that never existed
- **THEN** the refusal is identical to the refusal for a revoked link

#### Scenario: The endpoint is switched off

- **WHEN** the public endpoint is disabled
- **THEN** every link is refused identically, with no deployment having occurred

### Requirement: No receipt is offered for indexing, preview or reference

Every receipt response SHALL instruct crawlers not to index it and not to follow
from it, and SHALL NOT rely on a crawl exclusion that would prevent that
instruction being read.

Every receipt response SHALL suppress the onward transmission of its own URL as a
referrer.

Where a receipt link is fetched to build a preview card, the preview SHALL carry
no bill number, no amount and no item, so that the contents of a receipt do not
travel with a forwarded link.

#### Scenario: A search engine finds a receipt URL

- **WHEN** a crawler fetches a receipt link
- **THEN** it is served the instruction not to index it, having been allowed to
  fetch it in order to read that instruction

#### Scenario: A link is pasted into a chat

- **WHEN** a chat application fetches the link to build a preview
- **THEN** the preview identifies the business and says a receipt is there, and
  discloses no amount, item or bill number

### Requirement: Public traffic is bounded and observed

The public endpoint SHALL rate limit requests per client and SHALL enforce a
ceiling on total volume, refusing beyond either.

Every request that resolves to a bill SHALL be recorded with the token, the time,
a non-reversible representation of the client address, and the user agent, so that
a harvesting attempt is visible after the fact.

A request that does not resolve SHALL NOT cause a database write, so that a flood
of invalid tokens cannot be turned into a flood of writes. Such requests SHALL be
bounded and counted at the edge instead.

A recorded access SHALL NOT be capable of identifying the customer.

#### Scenario: A harvesting attempt

- **WHEN** a client makes a large volume of requests against tokens that do not
  resolve
- **THEN** the requests are refused beyond the limit, no database write occurs for
  any of them, and the volume is visible at the edge

#### Scenario: A customer opens their receipt twice

- **WHEN** a link is opened and its PDF then downloaded
- **THEN** the accesses are recorded against that link, identifying no person

### Requirement: A receipt link carries its token after a question mark

Every receipt link the app hands out SHALL take the form
`<receipt base>/bill?t=<token>`, so that everything before the `?` is identical for
every bill and the token is the only part that varies. In production that prefix is
`https://shawarmania.in/bill?`, the address registered with the telecom operator as
the sender's dynamic call-to-action URL, against which an SMS carrying the link is
validated.

The token SHALL be the link's only query parameter as handed out. A surface MAY
add a parameter of its own when it opens the link, such as the counter asking for
its view, and SHALL keep `t` unchanged when it does.

The receipt SHALL be served at that address, its counter view at the same address
with `view=counter`, and its PDF from an address of its own.

A request to `/bill` whose `t` is absent, empty, repeated or not of the token's
shape SHALL receive the same refusal as a token that resolves to no bill.

A request to `/bill/<token>` for a token of the right shape SHALL be redirected to
`/bill?t=<token>`, keeping any other query parameter, before any lookup is made, so
that the redirect discloses nothing about whether the bill exists.

#### Scenario: A link is handed out

- **WHEN** a bill's receipt link is sent, opened or viewed from the app
- **THEN** it reads `https://shawarmania.in/bill?t=<token>`, and opens that bill's
  receipt

#### Scenario: The counter views a receipt

- **WHEN** the counter opens a receipt in its own view
- **THEN** the address carries `t` and `view=counter`, and the counter view is
  served

#### Scenario: A link with its token mangled

- **WHEN** `/bill` is requested with no `t`, an empty `t`, two `t` parameters, or a
  `t` that is not a token's shape
- **THEN** the refusal is identical to the refusal for an unknown token

#### Scenario: A link in the old shape

- **WHEN** `/bill/<token>?view=counter` is requested
- **THEN** it redirects to `/bill?t=<token>&view=counter`, whether or not the token
  names a bill

#### Scenario: A filled-in receipt message

- **WHEN** the receipt message is filled with a ten-character token and a
  four-digit balance
- **THEN** it fits one 160-character GSM-7 SMS segment

### Requirement: The receipt states the points a bill earned, used and left

When a bill has points entries, the receipt SHALL show the points it used, the
points it earned, and the balance after it, each read from the bill's own stored
entries and never recomputed or read from the customer's current balance.

These figures SHALL NOT identify the customer, and the receipt SHALL continue to
name no customer.

A bill with no points entries SHALL show no points section.

#### Scenario: A bill that earned and used
- **WHEN** a bill that used 20 points and earned 5 is served, leaving 42
- **THEN** the receipt shows 20 used, 5 earned and a balance of 42, and nothing naming the customer

#### Scenario: The balance moves afterwards
- **WHEN** the same customer pays another bill and the first receipt is opened again
- **THEN** it still shows a balance of 42

#### Scenario: A bill from before points
- **WHEN** a bill rung before its outlet turned points on is served
- **THEN** no points section appears

### Requirement: The receipt says how the bill was served

The receipt SHALL say whether a bill was dine-in or takeaway when the bill recorded
it, read from the bill's own stored value.

The receipt SHALL NOT show a table number, and the reader SHALL NOT return one. A
table is a label for the length of a meal, like the day's order number, which the
receipt does not show either [owner, 2026-09-30].

A bill that recorded neither SHALL say nothing about how it was served.

#### Scenario: A dine-in bill at a table

- **WHEN** a bill recorded as dine-in at table 4 is served
- **THEN** the page, its counter view and the PDF say *Dine-in*, and no table
  appears anywhere in the payload or on the receipt

#### Scenario: A takeaway bill

- **WHEN** a bill recorded as takeaway is served
- **THEN** the receipt says takeaway

#### Scenario: A bill from before the choice existed

- **WHEN** a bill that recorded no service type is served
- **THEN** the receipt says nothing about how it was served
